We take the position.

Meridiam Maritime is an integrated short-sea operator working 3,000–5,000 DWCC tonnage across the European ranges.

Founded by Spanish and Portuguese principals in partnership with Pérez y Cía, a maritime group established in 1853, and a Geneva shipping financier, Meridiam is a chartering, shipping and transport service provider concentrating in the short-sea dry cargo segment. We trade ARAG, UK Continent, North Sea, Irish Sea, Baltic, Mediterranean and Black Sea.

Meridiam is a fully integrated operator managing the full scope of shipping related activities — from the freight sale and the contract of affreightment, through the chartering of tonnage on our own account, down to an operations desk that handles every pre- and post-fixture aspect of the voyage through to final accounts. Nothing is passed to a third party.

Three people, thirty years on the European short-sea markets between them, and shareholder capital behind the book rather than third-party working capital.

What this means for you

Two counterparties. Two commitments.

For shipowners

Hire on the day. Every time.

Your ship is employed by a principal capitalised by its shareholders, not by an intermediary passing it on to a third party whose credit you have never seen. Period and trip employment, planned rotations, and a desk that keeps the ship working rather than idle between fixtures.

  • Hire paid on the due date, without reminder
  • Shareholder facilities behind the working capital
  • Bunkers procured and paid by us on TC-in tonnage
  • Employment planned to minimise ballast legs
For cargo owners

A rate that holds.

We take the tonnage risk before you take the price. Once terms are agreed the number is ours to defend, whatever the market does between fixture and loading. The desk that fixed your cargo executes it through to final accounts.

  • Firm rates, held through market movement
  • Contracts of affreightment across a season
  • One desk from enquiry to demurrage settlement

Chartering & operations

The full scope, under one desk.

Freight sale, tonnage procurement, voyage execution and settlement are handled by the same three people. Nothing is passed on.

01

Freight sale and cargo contracting

Spot freight and contracts of affreightment priced against tonnage we control.

02

Time chartering on own account

Trip and period tonnage taken in as principal, staggered so no single market point rolls the book.

03

Bunker procurement

Purchased and paid by Meridiam on TC-in tonnage, with quality and quantity attended at the stem.

04

Port call and agency

Agents appointed and supervised, proforma DAs verified against our shareholder's network.

05

Laytime and final accounts

Laytime calculated, demurrage claimed and settled, statements issued on a fixed schedule.
